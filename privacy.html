Faras — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Summary

Faras does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your display name, avatar, virtual chip balance, game statistics and settings are stored only on your device (using iOS app storage). Deleting the app deletes this data.

Playing with others

  • Play Nearby connects directly to nearby devices over your local network or Bluetooth. Only your display name, avatar, chip count and game moves are sent, and only to players in your room. Connections are encrypted.
  • Online uses Apple Game Center. Apple handles sign-in and matchmaking under Apple's Privacy Policy. Faras sends your display name, avatar, chip count and game moves to the players in your match.

Virtual chips

Chips are for entertainment only. They cannot be purchased, have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes. Faras does not offer real-money gambling.

Children

Faras is not directed at children under 17 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
