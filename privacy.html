Faras — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Summary

Faras does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your display name, avatar, game statistics and settings are stored only on your device (using iOS app storage). Deleting the app deletes this data.

Playing with others

  • Play Nearby connects directly to nearby devices over your local network or Bluetooth. Only your display name, avatar, score and game moves are sent, and only to players in your room. Connections are encrypted.
  • Online uses Apple Game Center. Apple handles sign-in and matchmaking under Apple's Privacy Policy. Faras sends your display name, avatar, score and game moves to the players in your match.

No money involved

Faras is played with free points only. Every match starts with the same free stack; points cannot be bought, cashed out or exchanged for money or prizes, and they reset every match. There is no real-money gambling and nothing to buy.

Children

Faras is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.
