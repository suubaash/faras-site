Faras – Teen Patti & Flush

The classic three-card game (Teen Patti, Flush, 3 Patti) for iPhone and iPad, played for points — for all ages. No betting, no ads, nothing to buy.

Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas: suubaash@live.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

How does scoring work? Each hand you can stay or fold. Folding early banks safe points; the best hand at the reveal scores its hand points for every opponent beaten, doubled if you never looked at your cards. Most points after the match wins.

Play Nearby can't find my friend's room. Both phones must be on the same Wi-Fi (or close together with Bluetooth on), and Faras needs the Local Network permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

Online says Game Center is unavailable. Sign in under Settings → Game Center on your device.

How do I change the language? Faras follows your device language (English, 한국어, नेपाली, हिन्दी). On iOS you can also pick it per app under Settings → Faras → Language.
